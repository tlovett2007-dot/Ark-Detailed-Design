Observations

The transmission pattern is clear:

  1. The chain is BDB → everyone. Almost every post-1906 source that claims geometric meaning cites BDB or inherits it through a commentary that cites BDB. BDB itself cites Erman (1900) and Gesenius.
  2. Cohen (1972) is the decisive intervention. He surveyed all proposals and found none sufficient to establish etymology. Every source that asserts geometric meaning after 1972 without engaging Cohen is citing a claim that has been philologically demolished.
  3. Cohen is frequently cited but never engaged. Wenham (1987), Spoelstra (2013), and others list Cohen in footnotes but do not address his conclusions. Citing a demolition paper without engaging it is worse than not citing it — it implies awareness of the problem while refusing to deal with it.
  4. Cassuto's "precisely" is the only geometric assertion with any scholarly pretension — and it cites Haupt (1927) which is about the Babylonian vessel, not tebah etymology. The geometric claim itself is unreferenced.
  5. Strong's Concordance is the popular transmission vector — "a box" enters millions of Bible readers through Strong's, which simply inherits BDB uncritically.
