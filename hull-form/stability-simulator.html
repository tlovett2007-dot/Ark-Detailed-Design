Algorithm

Geometry engine

Each cross-section is defined by six parameters: keel height above datum, dead rise angle, bilge radius, side flare angle, deck edge coordinates, and deck camber angle. The half-section is built as a polyline: keel → bottom panel → bilge arc (tessellated) → side panel → deck edge → deck centreline. The bilge arc centre is computed as the reflection of the sharp corner across the T1→T2 chord (tangent point intersection method). The full section is the port/starboard mirror.

Hydrostatics

Submerged area and centroid are computed by the shoelace (Green's theorem) formula on the waterline-clipped polygon. Waterline clipping uses a standard Sutherland-Hodgman segment intersection. All integration is exact for the piecewise-linear polyline — no further approximation.

Constant displacement heeling

At each heel angle the hull is rotated about G, then shifted vertically by bisection (48 iterations, machine precision) until the submerged area equals the upright target. The waterline remains fixed in world space. GZ is the horizontal distance from B to G's vertical line.

GZ curve and AR

GZ is computed at 2° intervals for display and at 0.25° intervals for integration. AR is the trapezoid integral of GZ from 0° to φ_lim, where φ_lim = atan(freeboard / half-beam) — the angle at which the deck edge reaches the waterline. The shaded area on the GZ curve panel corresponds to AR.

Key findings to date

Hong et al. (1994) used KG = D/3, not the average of their two stated loading cases (which would give 4.57m). The round number KG = D/3 reproduces their Table 3 results exactly.

The box hull (zero deadrise) has low initial GM — the GZ curve rises slowly from zero, giving a long natural roll period. This is comfortable for occupants and cargo. Increased deadrise steepens the initial GZ slope and shortens the roll period, increasing snap-roll severity. The Genesis box proportions are not merely adequate — they are near-optimal for passenger and cargo comfort in an unpowered drifting vessel.

The simulator is general — it handles any combination of deadrise, flare, bilge radius, and camber. Hong's analytical formula applies only to rectangular sections. The next step is to apply the validated integrator to the actual Lovett/AiG hull form (Cb = 0.87, CAD file AR-003-20110418) and compute AR for the real hull — a number not previously published.
