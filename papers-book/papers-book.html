Publishing Strategy — Multiple Focused Books

The research programme generates enough material for a single comprehensive volume — but a series of focused books serves readers better and sells more copies. A $180 academic hardcover sits on the shelf; four $25 paperbacks get read, gifted, and recommended.

Different books reach different audiences who would never buy a single comprehensive volume:

BookProposed titlePrimary audienceContent
1 Noah Didn't Build an Ark Biblical scholars, linguists, apologists Word studies — tebah, gopher, kopher, cubit, deck terminology. The language of the Genesis specification.
2 Inconvenient Evidence: The Naval Architecture of Noah's Ark Engineers, naval architects, technical readers Hull form, structure, keels, weathervaning, seakeeping, Hong revisited. The engineering of the vessel.
3 TBD — The Voyage General Christian readers, creation science Animals, food, water, passive systems, capacity, flood phases. Life aboard the Ark.
4 TBD — The Evidence Apologists, academics, sceptics Bayesian arguments, probability, Genesis vs Gilgamesh, divine specification, motivated scepticism.

The paper series establishes credibility first. Each paper is independently publishable and contributes to the record. The books synthesise the papers for their respective audiences. A reader who wants the technical detail goes to the papers; a reader who wants the argument goes to the books.

Publication sequence: Papers first. Approach publishers after Papers 2 and 8 are in print. The paper series is both the scholarly foundation and the credibility signal that distinguishes these books from popular creationist literature.

Book 1 — Noah Didn't Build an Ark

Status: Papers in development — see tebah, gopher wood, kopher

The provocative title does the work — nobody built an "Ark" because that English word imported a box-shape assumption from Latin arca that isn't in the Hebrew tebah. The book is a series of word studies showing that the Genesis specification carries no geometric information in its vocabulary — the shape comes from the engineering, not the words.

The Narrative Spine — Motivated Scepticism

The unifying thread: motivated scepticism toward inconvenient evidence explains the persistent resistance to taking the Genesis account seriously as an engineering document. Three historical cases establish the pattern:

CaseInconvenient evidenceMotivated response
Marco Polo (13th century)Chinese junks with watertight bulkheads, multi-layer planking, balanced rudders — centuries ahead of European shipbuildingEuropeans dismissed him as a fabulist. The engineering was real.
Wellhausen (1878)A unified Mosaic authorship would imply the events of Genesis actually happenedDocumentary hypothesis — JEDP source dissection — removes the inconvenience by denying the document's coherence
Gilgamesh priorityGenesis proportions are optimal; Gilgamesh proportions are absurd — if Genesis copied Gilgamesh it somehow corrected every engineering error simultaneouslyDirection of dependence asserted without engaging the engineering evidence

Books 3 and 4 — The Voyage and The Evidence

Status: Concept only — content development follows Books 1 and 2

Book 3 — The Voyage: Life aboard the Ark. How many animals, what did they eat, how was waste managed, how did 8 people cope for 371 days. The passive systems that made it possible. Genesis 7-8 as a ship's log. Accessible to general readers — the human and animal story of the voyage.

Book 4 — The Evidence: The probability argument. Bayesian assessment of the Genesis dimensions against naval architecture constraints. The Genesis vs Gilgamesh comparison. Divine specification as engineering boundary condition. The motivated scepticism argument in its most rigorous form. For apologists, academics, and serious sceptics who want the full case.
